Regime = 20-day cumulative-move Markov (Equity ±5%, FX ±2%, Rates ±5%, Crypto ±10% → Up/Down, else Sideways); signal = P(up)−P(down) next day, honest stride-sampled matrix ("thin" = under 5 past samples of the current state; treat as low confidence). FVG = open 3-candle daily gaps, last 200 bars; open until a daily close beyond the far edge (matches Nephew_Sam). All rows: TradingView daily bars, full history (futures = front-month continuous incl. overnight session; FX = OANDA spot; yields = TVC with OHLC). Regime uses all history loaded (bar count per row). Fallback per row: IBKR futures, then yfinance/FRED; * = yfinance cash proxy. Rates — yield rows: UP = yields rising = bonds selling off; US Treasury futures rows (ZT/ZF/ZN/ZB) are price: UP = bond price up = yields down. Yield regimes only use history after the last time the yield was ≤0.25% (DE/JP/UK went negative 2016-21), so those rows rest on fewer samples. FX = spot._
